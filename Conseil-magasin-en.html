Retail Store Optimization

IB Conseil supports retail stores in improving their organization, sales performance and customer experience through practical and results-oriented solutions.

Store organization & layout

We help retail stores optimize their layout, product placement and customer journey to create a smoother and more engaging experience.

The objective is to improve readability, customer flow and in-store efficiency while supporting sales growth.

In-store sales performance

We focus on key performance drivers such as merchandising, assortment management and sales activation.

Each recommendation is designed to sustainably increase turnover and improve store profitability.

Operational field support

IB Conseil works alongside store teams to ensure effective implementation of action plans.

Support can be provided on a short- or long-term basis, depending on objectives, challenges and operational needs.
